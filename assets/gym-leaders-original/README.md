# Original gym leader portraits

Four new leaders for a later league expansion. Their portraits are available by id. They do not have gyms, maps, or battles yet. Team and town notes are in `docs/original-gym-leaders.md`. Rowan, Lyra, Flint, and Sera keep their atlas portraits.

`trainer-portraits.js` loads each id on demand: WebP first, then PNG. These are not part of the classic atlas.

## Characters

| ID | Name | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `gl_tamsin` | Tamsin | Gym Leader · Ground |
| `gl_orrin` | Orrin | Gym Leader · Psychic |
| `gl_corwin` | Corwin | Gym Leader · Ghost |
| `gl_kaelan` | Kaelan | Gym Leader · Dragon |

## Formats

Each ID has a 256×256 transparent `{id}.png` and a matching `{id}.webp`.
