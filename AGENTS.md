# Greenwood project instructions

- This is the Pkmngame repository. Never change or deploy Wayfarer's Lantern as part of a Greenwood change.
- Trainers and Gym Leaders must use the detailed painted chibi explorer art family. Render NPCs through `trainer-portraits.js`; it supplies matching male/female fallbacks for future NPCs. Add a unique matching portrait for named leaders. Do not replace these with simple geometric avatar art.
- Keep existing save IDs, collection individuals, Shiny variants, party order and saved trainer appearance when migrating.
- Mega and Gigantamax are temporary battle transformations, never permanent species replacements. Use the pinned simulator, matching stones, Max Soup and the explicit Generation 8 ruleset for Gigantamax.
- Supply correct form-specific artwork. Do not silently reuse a base-species picture as a Mega/Gigantamax form. Use clearly labelled canonical pixel sprites if a painted chibi is unavailable.
- Run `npm test` and stage with `npm run stage`. Build dependencies and scratch files must not be deployed. Hosting remains the free static GitHub Pages workflow.

- Every painted chibi must use a matching canonical Shiny palette when the individual is Shiny; never substitute its Normal palette or a different art style. Every newly added Pokémon or battle form must ship with matching painted, full-body chibi art for BOTH Normal and canonical Shiny palettes. Include it in the gallery, collection, follower and motion-off battle render paths. Use the existing painted style; do not call pixel sprites finished chibi artwork. `tests/world-polish.test.mjs` enforces coverage for entries beyond the explicitly frozen legacy roster. Preserve true alpha transparency, never a baked-in checkerboard.
- Areas within a region must have distinct authored geography, individual colour treatment and contextual scenery/landmarks; do not rotate a shared map template for multiple areas. Keep all gates, Gym/Center entrances, hunt clues and saved positions safely reachable.

- Shiny variants must be direct material-specific colour edits of the approved Normal chibi, preserving its exact pose, silhouette and alpha. Do not regenerate or redesign a Shiny sprite. Keep white teeth, eyes and prismatic crystals separate from metal recolouring.

- The supplied Mega Raichu Y, Gigantamax Gengar and Gigantamax Pikachu images are references for painted chibi generation. Mega Raichu Y now has paired painted art. Gigantamax Pikachu now also has paired painted art. Gigantamax Gengar now also has paired painted art. All current species/forms must render their matching painted pair in chibi views. Shiny variants must recolour the matching Normal drawing.

- Kanto Leaders uses two extra Greenwood exhibition maps, separate from each region’s five-area loop. Keep the courtyard entrance, hall exit and all 14 trainers reachable. Jump to map must include every destination, preserve saves and grant no free battle rewards or healing. Trainer wins use stable `kanto-*` IDs.
- Kanto portraits are supplied in `assets/trainers-kanto/`. Use individual WebP images with PNG error fallbacks via `trainer-portraits.js`, keeping Brock and all original atlas indices intact. Canonical asset IDs and existing `kanto-*` aliases are supported. Keep saved trainer IDs stable; Lt. Surge remains `kanto-surge` with portrait `lt-surge`.

- Pokémon presentation dimensions live in `pokemon-size-data.js` and `pokemon-sizing.js`. Refresh the former with `node scripts/build-pokemon-sizes.mjs` for new species/forms. Keep relative size bounded for mobile, matching between Normal/Shiny and separate from combat stats. Map trainer/leader name labels must not show exclamation marks.

- Alakazam is directly catchable as a rare Observatory Woods encounter, with canonical stats/moves and paired painted artwork. Kadabra is also catchable there, with paired art and Linking Cord evolution into Alakazam. Abra and the other pending draft species remain unregistered until their artwork is available. Preserve Alakazam captures and the stable `alakazam` ID.

- Pokémon artwork is chibi-only. Fresh and imported saves normalize to `artStyle: home` and `artwork: true`; these fields remain for save compatibility, not selectable styles. Use `chibiSprite`/`chibiSources` in gameplay, starter selection, battles, followers, gallery and evolution library. No model/official/pixel art fallback may replace a missing painted image. Keep Normal/Shiny palette selection and the independent battle/capture motion toggle. Preserve the newer trainer portrait manifest/gallery and its assets.

- Venomoth is directly catchable in Greenwood/Fernwood Canopy (Fog/Dusk favoured); Tentacruel in Greenwood/Moonlit Shore (Rain/Night favoured). Both require matching painted Normal/Shiny art. Preserve their canonical stats and stable IDs, and keep the newer mobile touch controls from main.

- User approved adding the remaining Larvitar, Bagon, Gible, Frigibax and Jangmo-o lines before all artwork is ready. The explicit IDs in `artwork-status.js` may use clearly labelled temporary Normal/Shiny illustrations in every view. They must never be labelled painted chibis or silently fall back to models/pixel art. All other species/forms still require painted pairs. Remove a pending ID only when its matching Normal/Shiny pair is bundled; replacing art must preserve saved individuals.

- User explicitly approved registering all original National #001–151 species with labelled placeholders where no chibi pair exists. `kanto151-data.js` holds the canonical additions and encounter sites. Preserve existing artwork and saved individuals; `artwork-status.js` marks missing pairs until both painted registry entries are supplied. Do not label placeholders as finished art.

- Gym Leader career is the default mode from v0.37.0. User authorised a new personal-Gym economy and challenger loop. Keep `greenwood.gym.v1` fully separate from the legacy `greenwood.save.v2` adventure save, reachable at `?mode=adventure`. Defence rewards settle exactly once after the entire challenger team; no coin reward for withdrawal/defeat and no charges for recovery. Keep entrances, court and central aisle clear when placing cosmetic decorations. Elite Four/Champion and multiplayer/puzzles/hired trainers remain explicitly future systems.

- From v0.38.0, Gym careers require a chosen type before new battles. Build the simulator side only from matching permanent species in the active party (either dual type qualifies). Preserve off-type collection individuals and party order; they cannot swap into defences/exhibitions. Type changes are blocked during a challenge. The trainer directory includes every uploaded leader/Elite Four/Champion; exhibitions award first-win coins once and store `gym.leagueWins`, without promoting the player's career or changing ordinary defence reputation/counters.

- From v0.39.0 the personal Gym and developing town share one 15×15 map. Preserve old 9×9 decoration coordinates and render them with a +3 town offset. Construction uses fixed plots and three saved levels per project in `gym.town.projects`; do not overwrite careers or adventure saves. Keep map characters at town scale, plot controls readable, the central road reachable and costs/reputation enforced. Town buildings are cosmetic until a later user-authorised mechanics change.

- From v0.40.0, Gym mode has walkable town/hall/breeding/reserve maps in `gym-world-data.js`. Challenges take place in the hall. Breeding is only a clearly labelled preview. Reserve purchases enter holding; release/capture must preserve each individual UID and variant/training, and remove exactly one resident on capture. Flee/KO retains residents; no repeat cash payouts or held-item duplication. Preserve one-time capture supplies, active reserve battles, town upgrades and all existing careers; adventure saves remain separate. Personal-map movement uses `gym.world`, not adventure `region/area/position` fields.

- Walking motion uses the existing customised directional atlas and `world-motion.js`; preserve the trainer design and colours. `mapMotion` is independent of battle/capture `motion`, with device reduced-motion taking priority. Keep character motion transient, and use the saved challenger arrival timestamp so map redraws cannot restart an approach.

Current user direction supersedes the old one-screen town requirement: town is 39×39 with a follow camera. Legacy town coordinates offset by +12 exactly once (world.version=2); local decoration coordinates remain saved unchanged and render at +15. Preserve fixed authored terrain and project locations.

- Gym design is stored in `gym.design`, validated by `gym-design.js`. Preserve the shared interior/exterior emblem and all custom colours/materials/furnishings across imports and upgrades. Furniture placement must protect doors, the central aisle and court; hall collision must use the saved design. Cosmetic changes are free, blocked during challenges, and must not change party eligibility, career currency/reputation, trainer appearance or battle stats.

- Personal Gym shopping lives in the walkable town shop (`shop` map), with items, Pokémon and decorations as in-building departments rather than main navigation tabs. Preserve its town doorway (10,17), exit to town (10,18), saved shop location and the Visit shop convenience action. Keep the legacy adventure Market separate. Developer coin/reputation edits target one balance only; they must never clear inventory, individual creatures, town/design or match records. Keep the broader items-and-gold reset separately labelled.

- The shop purchase menu must remain hidden until talking to the shopkeeper near the north counter (5,5), and close on refresh, Escape or explicit dismissal. Entry/shortcuts must not auto-open catalogues. Keep the painted room, protected counter, transient dialog, keyboard/touch interaction, and south exit (5,10) aligned; shop spawn/return-to-exit staging is (5,9). Preserve purchase data and scroll during menu updates.

- Rich Gym architecture/materials are layered by `gym-interior-art.js`; preserve editable emblems, floor/wall/carpet/trim colours and furniture. `showArchitecture` defaults on for old saves and is a cosmetic toggle only. Keep the architecture centre transparent and exits unblocked. Small interiors can show the full room while town remains scrolling.

- Do not reintroduce standalone entrance arches or benches in the Gym. `showDoors` remains only for save compatibility and normalises false; old bench placements are discarded without affecting other furnishings/progress. Keep the central court markings and the custom Gym crest on its approach, preserving exterior-emblem linkage and all entrance routes.
