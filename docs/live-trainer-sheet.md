# Live trainer teams (v0.69)

The free public trainer configuration is [this Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RPZC7zcc6_5lOpPGd94Vdz_o6XV-hx-1b3AF2YET5QE/edit). Its Trainers tab contains all 249 uploaded directory characters plus eight defence challenger profiles, in 1,779 editable reputation-band rows. Species supplies the 1,262 permanent species IDs. This does not modify Wayfarer's Lantern.

Edit the live Google Sheet, not an exported Excel copy. Use Settings → Live trainer teams → Sync now to check changes; the open game otherwise checks every 60 seconds while visible. Google can briefly cache exports. No sign-in, paid service or secret is bundled. The sheet must retain Anyone with the link → Viewer access; only game configuration belongs here, never player/private information.

Each row specifies Trainer ID, Name, Kind, inclusive Min/Max reputation, six species/level pairs, and Enabled. A blank maximum is unlimited. IDs and Kind are stable references. Use the Species tab's IDs, levels 1–100, occupied slots in order, and non-overlapping reputation ranges per trainer. Disable rows with the checkbox rather than deleting a trainer entirely. Duplicate a row and adjust both ranges to introduce a new band. All known trainers must retain at least one row. A trainer with no enabled row at the current reputation cannot appear or be challenged. Keep at least one enabled Defence row at reputation zero. To disable every defence at a later reputation intentionally, the invitation explains the missing configuration instead of starting an empty match.

Gym defence uses the configured eligible profiles, concealed teams, matching-type defenders and the opponent-count cap. Gallery identities still rotate daily and require their room upgrades, but teams/names/individual levels come from this sheet at battle acceptance. Newly disabled guests disappear; newly enabled identities enter the next daily selection. Reputation edits change future team eligibility immediately on the next render/battle. Species are used exactly as entered: no automatic evolution overrides your choices. Only actual Gym defence changes reputation; Gallery rewards remain coins only.

AI remains game-owned: Rookie below 60 reputation, Journeyman at 60–299 and Ace at 300+. The existing strategic engine chooses attacks/switches; stronger tiers receive legal move coverage, complementary natures and trained IVs/EVs. Sheet edits do not affect players' creatures, rewards, AI code, facilities or saves.

`trainer-feed.js` reads Google's public CSV with no credentials, parses quoted fields, validates the entire candidate snapshot and caches only valid rows under `gymbound.trainer-sheet.v1`. Invalid/offline/timeout responses retain the last good configuration; the shipped `trainer-teams-data.js` is the fallback for a fresh/offline installation. Cache errors never clear a career. Settings reports the source, last successful sync and validation error. Active and already accepted challenges snapshot their species/levels, so later edits never rewrite an opponent mid-match, including reloads.

`battle.js` accepts per-slot `teamLevels` while retaining legacy `level` compatibility for old in-progress challenges. Other matches such as wild encounters, Arena practice and the separate legacy adventure use their own existing team systems. The spreadsheet controls the Gym career's defence profiles and uploaded Visitor's Gallery characters.

Export the bundled backup for Excel with `node scripts/export-trainer-sheet.mjs`; optionally pass an existing public CSV and output path to validate/export it. To change the fallback for future offline installs, validate a downloaded CSV with `trainersFromCSV`, then replace the exported `trainerTeamDefaults` with the validated rows and publish normally. Online edits need no GitHub commit. A later backend can replace this read-only adapter without changing battle snapshots or player storage.
